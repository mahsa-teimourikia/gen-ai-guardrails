# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in GenAI Guardrails a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give specific, constructive technical feedback.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and requests to stop.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, or publishing private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the project publicly, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate it and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through [@mahsa-teimourikia's GitHub profile](https://github.com/mahsa-teimourikia). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.
